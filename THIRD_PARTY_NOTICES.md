The seven SVGs in `public/aws` are unchanged AWS Architecture Icons from Amazon Web Services’ July 31, 2026 icon package, downloaded from the [official architecture icon page](https://aws.amazon.com/architecture/icons/). The EC2, RDS, S3, SQS, ElastiCache, and CloudFront icons are from the package’s 48px architecture service set. The Application Load Balancer icon is from its networking resource set. Their use illustrates hypothetical systems and does not imply endorsement.
